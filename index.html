The social aspect of the human person refers to our natural need and capacity to connect, communicate, and form relationships with others. Humans are not isolated beings; our identities, values, and beliefs are shaped through our interactions and communities. While these connections give us belonging and help us grow, they can also influence us to conform to social expectations. Thus, being social involves balancing our need for connection with our need for individuality and authenticity.
